OUR SOLUTION: POSTGRESQL NATIVE SEARCH

WHY POSTGRESQL?

✅ Zero licensing cost – Open-source, production-proven

✅ Superior matching – 3 modern strategies vs. 5 legacy algorithms
   • Exact matching (normalized, handles business suffixes)
   • Fuzzy matching (pg_trgm - catches typos, spelling variations)
   • Phonetic matching (Soundex, Metaphone, Double Metaphone - sounds-alike names)

✅ Blazing fast – Sub-100ms queries on billions of records

✅ Handles all complexity – Nicknames, hyphens, transliterations, multicultural names
